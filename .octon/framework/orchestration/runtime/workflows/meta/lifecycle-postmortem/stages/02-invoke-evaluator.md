# Invoke Evaluator

Use the lifecycle-postmortem evaluator template to prepare an evaluator input
packet from the retained evidence map and known limits.

The evaluator input must require:

- evidence-grounded lifecycle reconstruction;
- the full eighteen-section postmortem report structure from the evaluator
  template;
- intended lifecycle job, what-went-well, what-did-not-go-well,
  Chesterton's Fence, complexity, and constraint analysis;
- at least four alternative improvement paths;
- complete 0-5 lifecycle quality attribute scoring;
- invariant compliance review before quality scoring;
- invariant validity and evolution review after redesign pressure and before
  final recommendations;
- patch-versus-redesign reasoning;
- final judgment from the allowed enum;
- concrete postmortem closeout actions;
- explicit non-authority statement.

Evaluator output is retained evidence only. It cannot authorize lifecycle
transition, closeout, promotion, redesign, support widening, generated-output
publication, or invariant amendment.
