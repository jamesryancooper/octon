# Validation

The discovery audit is complete because:

- target mode is explicitly observed;
- the exact-file surface is fully accounted with no unaccounted files;
- every critical claim has path-level evidence, impact, risk, and acceptance criteria;
- recommendations name priority, benefit, and tradeoff;
- keep-as-is decisions and unknowns are explicit;
- self-challenge rejects the expectation-only receipt and predictable-PR interpretations;
- the done gate truthfully remains revision-required.
