# Authoritative Promotion Prerequisites

This reconciliation is research and decision input only. It is non-authoritative, non-implementing, and non-promoting.

It does not become Octon authority until all of the following are complete:

1. Current-state reverification is complete at the exact implementation/promotion commit, including read-only live provider evidence.
2. Operator dispositions are recorded for every genuine decision in `remaining-operator-decisions.yml`.
3. Formal ADRs or equivalent accepted decision records preserve, amend, or explicitly reopen the relevant FD baseline.
4. Authoritative contracts, schemas, registries, and source ownership are updated and validated.
5. The proof-of-architecture obligations in `proof-obligations.yml` pass with direct, correctly classified evidence.
6. Adversarial and fault-injection tests pass for authority, isolation, store, broker, Git, verifier, evidence, recovery, trust activation, extensions, and children.
7. Every proposal packet closes with its required receipts, rollback proof, and no unresolved blocker.
8. Support claims are reverified against the complete supported vertical, provider configuration, and solo burden budgets.
9. Independent reproduction confirms the decisive security, recovery, provider, and product claims.
10. Promotion receipts are produced through Octon's authoritative decision and publication process.

Failure of a prerequisite must demote or block the affected claim/route. It must not be converted into a documentation-only exception or an unsafe compatibility bridge.
