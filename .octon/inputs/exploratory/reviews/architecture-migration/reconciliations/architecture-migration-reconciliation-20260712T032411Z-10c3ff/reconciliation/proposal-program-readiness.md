## Program structure

- Fourteen workgroups preserve the accepted migration order.
- Fifteen proposal packets provide exclusive source ownership; RP-12 extensions and RP-13 children are separate even though RWG-12 coordinates them.
- RP-11 owns the generic executor-adapter interface; RP-06 owns verifier/publication specialization; RP-13 owns child mapping; RP-14 owns claim-level cross-provider proof only.
- No packet owns a second authority source, store, broker, verifier/effect identity, or self-authorizing metadata.

For the solo operator, these remain internal delivery partitions. Present them as five value milestones, not fifteen product concepts:

1. containment;
2. trusted execution (RP-01 through RP-04);
3. safe delivery and recovery (RP-05 through RP-08);
4. self-development and multi-project harness (RP-09 through RP-11);
5. optional extensions/children and claim-scoped promotion (RP-12 through RP-14).

The core Solo Local claim may be promoted after its own RP-08/RP-09/RP-10/RP-11 proof. Signed-extension, child-agent, secondary-provider, and safe-automatic trust claims remain disabled/unclaimed until their own proof closes. Full program closeout still waits for all accepted packets.
