# Acceptance Criteria

- A receipt contract exists for feature-catalog drift findings.
- The gate has explicit no-change, documented-change, documented-retirement,
  and blocked-unresolved-drift outcomes.
- The gate classifies additions, removals, retirements, renames, splits,
  merges, status changes, material boundary updates, stale refs, and authority
  note drift.
- The gate is defined as evidence-only and non-authorizing.
