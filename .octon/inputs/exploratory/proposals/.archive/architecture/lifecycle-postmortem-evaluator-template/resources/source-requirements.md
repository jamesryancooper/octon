# Source Requirements

- Preserve the original postmortem's analytical depth.
- Require final lifecycle fitness judgment from the allowed set.
- Require patch-versus-redesign reasoning before recommendations.
- Include Octon invariant evaluation when the lifecycle concerns Octon.
- Place invariant evaluation before quality scoring.
- Evaluate invariant preservation, structural enforcement, and retained
  evidence.
- Use only Pass, Partial, Fail, Unknown, and Not Applicable for invariant
  ratings.
- Treat Unknown as an evidence gap, never as Pass.
- Require blocking or corrective findings for material invariant failures.
- Include a separate invariant validity and evolution review after redesign
  pressure and before final recommendations.
- Evaluate whether each invariant remains correct, complete, enforceable,
  evidenceable, well-scoped, non-conflicting, non-duplicative, and useful.
- Record hack pressure and classification accuracy for each invariant.
- Use only Keep, Clarify, Strengthen, Relax, Split, Merge, Reclassify,
  Replace, Remove, and Add for validity/evolution recommendations.
- Record required change and change-control bar for every non-Keep
  recommendation.
- Keep invariant change recommendations non-authorizing until separately
  accepted through governance, proposal, or amendment routes.
- Connect major findings to evidence, lifecycle purpose, governance, authority,
  risk, quality attributes, or implementation reality.
- End with a concrete action plan, not vague retrospective advice.
- Keep recommendations non-authorizing until separately accepted.
