# Source Of Truth Map

- `proposal.yml`: child manifest and scope.
- `architecture-proposal.yml`: architecture framing.
- `architecture/target-architecture.md`: proposed escalation policy.
- `architecture/implementation-plan.md`: future implementation sequence.
- `architecture/acceptance-criteria.md`: packet acceptance criteria.
- `resources/source-context.md`: parent postmortem context.
- `support/implementation-run.md`: promoted escalation policy implementation receipt.
- `support/implementation-conformance-review.md`: conformance review for hard-blocker escalation boundaries.
- `support/post-implementation-drift-churn-review.md`: drift and churn review for escalation policy updates.
- `support/proposal-closeout.md`: child-owned closeout receipt, archive
  authorization, hygiene evidence, and durable promotion evidence refs.

Parent program evidence may summarize this child but cannot change escalation
policy until durable implementation lands.
