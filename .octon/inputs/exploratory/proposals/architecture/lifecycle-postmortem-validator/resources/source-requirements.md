# Source Requirements

- Validate that every major finding connects to evidence or a declared known
  limit.
- Validate final judgment categories exactly.
- Validate postmortem sections are present.
- Validate invariant evaluation is present for Octon lifecycle subjects.
- Validate invariant ratings are exactly Pass, Partial, Fail, Unknown, or Not
  Applicable.
- Reject Unknown invariant ratings treated as Pass.
- Reject material invariant failures without evidence gap, blocking status, or
  required correction.
- Validate invariant validity/evolution review is present for Octon lifecycle
  subjects.
- Validate invariant validity/evolution recommendation categories exactly.
- Reject non-Keep recommendations without required change and change-control
  bar.
- Reject relaxation, removal, narrowing, downgrading, or addition
  recommendations without high or very high scrutiny.
- Reject reports that claim evaluator recommendations changed invariant
  authority.
- Reject generated and raw input authority drift.
- Reject reports that treat findings as accepted dispositions.
- Support direct command execution and workflow invocation.
- Retain validation evidence outside proposal paths.
