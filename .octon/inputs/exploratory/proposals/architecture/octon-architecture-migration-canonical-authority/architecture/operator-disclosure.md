the exact guard with no routine prompt or returns one concise denial describing
the failing identity/scope and the safe next action. RP-01 adds no credential,
provider, Git, or database ceremony.

content-addressed semantic epoch-zero inventory, a one-time human trust
anchor/bootstrap, and exact scope, artifact-version, time, budget, verifier,
health, and rollback preauthorization. Encoding, indirect-change detection,
health windows, canaries, and rollback durations remain reversible engineering
and proof work.

FD-018 and its proof gates still control activation. Safe automatic activation
remains disabled and unclaimed until proof and later promotion acceptance pass;
this packet does not enable it. Setup exposes evaluator version, policy digest,
current epoch, and guard health through existing diagnostic surfaces; monthly
maintenance should be negligible beyond later program budget proof.

self-development, extensions, children, and broad provider support remain
unsupported and disabled.
