# Acceptance Criteria

- Every postmortem recommendation is mapped to one child packet.
- Every child packet declares scope, promotion targets, validators, acceptance criteria, risk posture, and dependency gates.
- Child authority is preserved and parent summaries do not replace child receipts.
- Material side effects remain explicitly authorization-gated.
- Low-risk deterministic recovery routes are eligible for bounded autonomous handling only after the relevant child packets define and validate them.
- The lifecycle postmortem mechanism has a generic proposal-program delivery evaluation profile that can run for any completed proposal-program lifecycle and produce structured blocker taxonomy, autonomy, efficiency, delivery proof-chain, and recommendation backlog evidence.
- Branch-no-PR delivery remains PR-free, proof-locked, and strict.
