# Acceptance Criteria

- Fresh setup completes in at most 15 minutes and each project onboarding in at
  most 5 minutes.
- At least 18 of 20 admitted Class A/B missions complete autonomously; all 20
  have zero unauthorized effects and zero routine prompts.
- Median human intervention is at most 2 minutes and at least 50% below the
  measured supervised baseline; p50 mediation is at most 60 seconds excluding
  tests and provider queue time.
- Eligible no-PR delivery is faster than the protected-PR comparison and
  p50/p95 end-to-end and mediated latency are reported under an equal
  substantive policy/harness/check floor.
- False eligible A/B-to-PR escalation, review-required PR bypass, and
  invalid/collision/`UNKNOWN`-to-PR conversion are each zero.
- Candidate-held provider credentials, privileged candidate-head execution,
  unauthorized effects, and routine prompts for eligible Class A/B are zero;
  expected-old CAS rejects every induced race without bypass.
- Broker recovery is at most 5 seconds with zero operator steps; every unknown,
  outage, retry, and recovery result remains truthful and work-preserving.
- The operator sees a one-screen summary with progressive disclosure; one Solo
  Local profile exposes no more than seven primary concepts.
- Raw/detail operational evidence committed to project Git is false; retained
  evidence growth and compaction remain within the child-defined quotas.
- Monthly administration is at most 15 minutes; proposal administration is at
  most 30 minutes per packet and at most 10% of implementation human time.
- Unsafe fallback is false and candidate work is preserved whenever
  publication is denied, collided, unavailable, failed, unknown, unmerged, or
  cleanup-deferred. Closed-unmerged deletion and false `cleaned` are zero.
- Canonical local `main` changes only after hosted landed proof as a
  fast-forward mirror. One curated commit is the automatic default and every
  admitted bounded multi-commit range is fully validated.
- UE-011, UE-014, and UE-015 are resolved with exact-commit evidence; FD-001,
  FD-023, and FD-024 promotion gates pass; the accepted ROD-006
  no-Octon-direct-main posture is bound.
- Signed-extension, child-agent, secondary-provider, and safe-automatic claims
  are promoted only when their specific dependencies and proof pass; otherwise
  they remain disabled and unclaimed.
