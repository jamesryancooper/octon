# Operator Disclosure

The intended experience is one Solo Local profile, at most seven concepts, a
single one-screen status, progressive detail, no routine prompts, automatic
recovery, and preserved work on blocked publication. RP-14 consumes the
accepted upstream RP-00 ROD-006 posture: Octon exposes no human or agent
direct-main route, ordinary human Git remains outside Octon, and protected PR
is the privileged bridge. Separately, the claim map and promotion handoff
summarize demonstrated claims, limits, burden, and residual risks for later
evidence-gated acceptance.

RP-14 cannot claim success by averaging away unauthorized effects, prompts,
false escalation, unsafe fallback, or missing evidence. Optional signed
extensions, child agents, secondary providers, and safe-automatic activation
remain visibly disabled/unclaimed until individually proved. Failed proof is a
useful product result, not permission to increase ceremony or add a control plane.
