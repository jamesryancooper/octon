# Operator Disclosure

## Intended Solo Experience

The operator states a mission intent in an adopted project. Octon resolves the
already-approved run inputs, compiles one exact Harness invisibly, authorizes
that digest, and launches through the admitted primary adapter. The operator
does not choose a packet, compiler, route bundle, adapter, executor string, or
provider module.

Normal status shows mission/run state and the useful next action. Compiler
source graphs, digests, adapter lifecycle details, and conformance identities
remain behind explicit diagnostic inspection.

## When Octon Blocks

A block is automatic when a source is missing, ambiguous, stale, invalid,
widened, or changed after compile; an adapter declaration is unknown or
drifted; the one-shot binding does not match; or the provider outcome is
unknown. Routine deterministic compilation and primary-adapter launch require
no operator confirmation.

The failure message reports:

- affected project, mission, and run;
- the first conflicting source or adapter identity in operator-meaningful
  terms;
- whether the provider started and whether its outcome is known;
- that candidate work and immutable inputs are preserved;
- whether unrelated runs remain usable; and
- the shortest safe recompile, repair, observe, cancel, or protected-route
  command.

It does not ask the operator to select a broader route/provider or expose
packet IDs, raw schema rows, tokens, secrets, or internal registry mechanics in
the normal path.

## Primary and Secondary Providers

The initial target proves one real primary provider. Existing code or manifest
material for another provider does not make it supported. A secondary appears
only after a separately proposed support tuple, current strict conformance, and
integrated promotion proof. Until then its launch is denied rather than offered
as a manual escape hatch.

## Failure and Recovery Expectations

Compilation failures happen before launch and preserve the exact source set.
Changed bindings require a fresh compile/authorization. Unknown provider
outcomes remain unknown and route to existing reconciliation; Octon never
labels success from desired-state coincidence or silently launches again.
Disabling the new path leaves work recoverable through the existing protected
route without restoring direct provider dispatch.

## Claim Honesty

This draft makes no current deterministic-Harness, autonomous-completion,
provider-replacement, secondary-provider, or support claim. RP-11 may later
produce PO-FD-020 and component conformance for PO-FD-023. Integrated provider
equivalence and Solo Local claims remain RP-14-owned and require current direct
proof.
