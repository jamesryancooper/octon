# Operator Disclosure

Ordinary Octon changes continue through the normal Class B/PR route with no new
prompt. Trust changes land safely but inert. Status shows active and candidate
version digests, classification reason, epoch, proof/health state, and available
certified rollback in one concise view.

ROD-003 is accepted and asks for no further operator choice. It fixes a small
content-addressed semantic epoch-zero inventory, a one-time human trust
anchor/bootstrap, and exact scope, artifact-version, time, budget, verifier,
health, and rollback preauthorization. Encoding and indirect-change detection,
health windows, canaries, and rollback durations remain reversible engineering
and proof work.

Safe automatic activation remains disabled and unclaimed until the separate
proof gate and later promotion acceptance pass. Failure restores the last
certified version automatically; no candidate self-certification or same-change
rule mutation is hidden.

Federation, distributed rollout, candidate self-certification, and unproved
safe-automatic activation remain unsupported.
