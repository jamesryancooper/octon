# Cutover Plan

## Cutover Profile

- release state: `pre-1.0`
- change profile: `atomic`
- atomic mode: `clean-break`
- safe default: mission child admission and launch disabled

The evidence stages below are inert or fake-provider stages. They do not create
two schedulers, two generic adapters, or two child identity models. Live child
launch activates only when the accepted ROD-005 baseline is bound to enforceable
provisional values and the exact contract, dependency, guard, isolation, limit,
cancellation, reconciliation, and retirement gates agree.

## Stage 0 — Configuration, Dependency, and Ownership Freeze

Bind the accepted ROD-005 baseline to conservative provisional values, attach
exact RP-08/RP-11 exit receipts, require the owning dependency's dynamic ED-001
premise proof, and assign every shared symbol and registry entry. Keep all
mission child launch disabled.

Safe resting point: existing single-agent RP-11 execution remains available;
no MissionChildRun can be admitted.

## Stage 1 — Inert Contracts and Deny-Default Policy

Publish strict MissionChildRun, child budget, provider mapping, retirement,
Harness/Agent Node references, registries, policy, and templates. Unknown
fields reject and the live mapping set remains empty.

Safe resting point: contracts can validate fixtures, but no guard, session,
candidate repository, provider task, or child identity can launch.

## Stage 2 — Dry Intersection and Budget Planning

Run strict parent/mission/project/Harness/role/isolation/provider/remaining-
budget intersection against generated fixtures. Produce admission or denial
evidence without consuming a guard or creating external/runtime resources.

Safe resting point: scope and budget decisions are inspectable; all output is
dry-run evidence and cannot become a launch request.

## Stage 3 — Staged Guard and Isolation Binding

Create disposable candidate/session fixtures through the already-proved
dependency and bind exact identities to an unconsumed one-shot guard. Exercise
credential, canonical-Git, sibling, widening, expiry, revocation, replay, and
TOCTOU negatives while provider launch remains disabled.

Safe resting point: staged resources are revocable and retireable; no provider
child exists and no durable effect is reachable.

## Stage 4 — Existing Scheduler With Fake Child Mapping

Map MissionChildRun jobs onto existing batch/lock/counter/retry/timeout/cancel
hooks and exercise fake success, failure, unknown, cancellation, usage,
retirement, and replacement. Prove ProgramChild semantics and scheduler census
unchanged.

Safe resting point: only fake mapping is admitted; the live primary mapping
remains disabled and single-agent execution remains the operational route.

## Stage 5 — Primary Mapping Shadow Proof

Exercise the separate primary child mapping with useful-positive and hostile
credential/session/depth/limit/cancel/unknown/retirement cases in an isolated,
non-publishing proof lane. RP-11's generic adapter and provider support policy
remain unchanged. RP-08 reconciles every unknown.

Safe resting point: conformance evidence exists, but production policy still
admits no live mapping.

## Stage 6 — Atomic Bounded-Child Activation

At one exact implementation and policy digest, admit the currently
conformance-proved mapping through the existing proof and promotion gates and
enable:

- exact strict-intersection admission;
- one-shot guard consumption and pre-spawn revalidation;
- fresh credentialless candidate/session binding;
- existing-scheduler hard ceilings and enforce-or-deny budget posture;
- exact provider/process cancellation and RP-08 unknown reconciliation; and
- output preservation followed by revocation, tombstone, and reuse denial.

Simultaneously make persistent child identity, credentials, canonical Git,
grandchildren/self-recruitment, direct durable effects, blind retry, and
process-exit-only retirement unreachable.

## Stage 7 — Retire Unsafe Claims and Close Evidence

Remove or correct claims that ProgramChild scheduling or token measurement
already satisfies FD-022. Refresh only owner-generated views, retain UE-013 and
component conformance evidence, run review/conformance/drift gates, and scan
for a second scheduler, generic-adapter mutation, credential exposure, or
proposal-path dependency.

## Prohibited States

- MissionChildRun is represented as ProgramChild, a persistent agent/account,
  or a reusable identity/session/home;
- any child can read/export credentials, access canonical Git/remotes/config,
  traverse parent/sibling control/evidence/candidate areas, publish, or invoke
  durable effects;
- missing or incomparable intersection input widens scope or budget;
- a child can spawn, recruit, delegate, or create a grandchild;
- measured tokens, cost, or evidence volume is labeled hard enforcement;
- a second scheduler, queue, worker, runtime/store, authority engine, broker,
  recovery controller, or generic adapter exists;
- lost/unknown provider outcome is retried, replaced, deleted, or inferred
  terminal before RP-08 reconciliation; or
- process exit is treated as retirement while a guard, session, provider task,
  candidate identity, lock, or child identity remains reusable.

## Promotion Handoff

After implementation proof, RP-13 supplies PO-FD-022 and its child-mapping
component contribution to PO-FD-023, with exact accepted ROD-005 configuration,
ED-001 premise, RP-08/RP-11, rollback, and UE-013 identities. RP-14 retains
integrated provider promotion proof. Proposal prose is never copied into runtime
authority.
