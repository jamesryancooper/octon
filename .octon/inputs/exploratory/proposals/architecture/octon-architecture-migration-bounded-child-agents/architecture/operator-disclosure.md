# Operator Disclosure

## Accepted Baseline And Engineering Configuration

ROD-005 accepts the lowest useful concurrency and conservative, adjustable
wall-clock, step, attempt/retry, token, cost, and evidence ceilings for Solo
Local. The exact launch-disabled values and enforcement classifications are
selected in `resources/mission-child-design-and-dependency-receipt.yml`; no
architecture decision remains open. They must be mechanically enforced where
declared hard, measured during dogfood, and widened one dimension at a time only
when retained evidence proves benefit. Any later operator-defined spending or
interruption cap is an additional governed configuration ceiling.

The fixed boundary is not optional: children are temporary, credentialless,
candidate-only, depth one, strictly narrower, exact-guarded, cancellable,
reconcilable, and irreversibly retired. Role templates and provider-native use
after conformance belong to the accepted model; engineering and retained proof
determine the supported templates, enforcement classification, and current
provider mapping admission.

Implementation must encode and prove the selected exact values and use measured
completion, queueing, usage, evidence growth, cancellation, and recovery results
to tune them. Until that implementation and every required dependency,
conformance, and proof gate are current, child launch remains disabled.

## Intended Solo Experience

Once configured, Octon may split eligible mission work across admitted children
without routine approval prompts. The operator continues to work through the
existing mission surface, which shows a compact summary for each child:

- role and scoped objective;
- queued/running/blocked/cancel/unknown/retiring/retired state;
- elapsed time and enforceable/measured remaining limits;
- output/candidate disposition;
- the first operator-meaningful block; and
- whether the parent can continue through the single-agent path.

No organization chart, agent account, persistent worker, queue administration,
provider credential setup, child repository management, or routine terminal
cleanup is introduced.

## Failure Presentation

Report the parent mission and temporary child role, the first failed gate,
whether the provider/process outcome is known or unknown, preserved output,
the exact safe system action in progress, and the shortest operator action if
one is truly required. Distinguish:

- denied before launch;
- cancelled with terminal outcome known;
- unknown and awaiting RP-08 reconciliation;
- output preserved but parent incorporation blocked; and
- retirement blocked by a named residual resource.

Do not expose prompts, credentials, environment/Keychain values, provider
session tokens, raw guard material, internal schema rows, or suggest widening
scope, disabling isolation, retrying unknown work, or reusing an identity.

## Cancel and Recovery Experience

One parent/mission cancel action targets exact admitted children, provider
tasks, and process groups. Octon preserves output/evidence, reports unknown
outcomes honestly, reconciles through RP-08, retires resources, and then shows
whether single-agent continuation is available. A replacement is shown as a
new child with predecessor lineage, never as a resumed old identity.

## Maintenance and Unsupported Surfaces

Normal maintenance is limited to the accepted ROD-005 configuration, current mapping
conformance, compact evidence, and rare blocked-retirement repair. Persistent
agents/accounts/organizations, named-role authority classes, reusable HOME or
session, credentials, canonical Git, direct publication/effects, depth above
one, self-recruitment, dynamic delegation, standing queues/workers, and
cross-mission children remain unsupported.

## Claim Honesty

This draft creates no current bounded-child, provider, multi-agent, or autonomy
support claim. FD-022 becomes eligible only after the accepted ROD-005
configuration is bound and proved, ED-001 dependency proof, RP-08/RP-11 exits,
PO-FD-022/UE-013 adversarial proof, RP-13's provider-mapping component
conformance, and claim-scoped RP-14 dogfood. Existing
ProgramChild scheduling and token measurement do not prove these outcomes.
